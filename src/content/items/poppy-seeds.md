Whole seeds won't bind with milk, so a filling made from them stays gritty, loose and flavourless. Grinding releases the oil and the taste.

**Grinding at home**

- **Coffee or spice grinder (kaffekværn):** the best option, from about 100–200 kr in Bilka. Grind one 50 g sachet at a time, in about 5 pulses of 3 seconds, shaking the grinder between pulses. A long run turns the seeds into a paste that sticks to the walls. They are done when they turn grey, smell strong and clump when pinched.
- If you also grind coffee, wipe the grinder first with a piece of dry bread or a spoon of rice. Coffee and poppy seed pass their taste to each other, so a grinder just for poppy seed and spices is ideal.
- **Stick blender:** soak or simmer the seeds in hot milk for about 30 minutes to soften them, then blend them in the pot. It is not as fine, but it works for koláče.
- **Mortar:** works, but it is a lot of effort for 200 g.
- A blender only spins the seeds around and doesn't grind them.
