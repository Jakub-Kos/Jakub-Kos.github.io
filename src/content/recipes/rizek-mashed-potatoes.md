1. **Boil the potatoes.** Peel the potatoes, cut them into chunks and boil them in salted water for about 20 minutes, until a knife slides in easily.

2. **Prepare the meat.** Meanwhile, pound the chops into thin cutlets (about 5 mm) between two sheets of cling film. Season both sides with salt and pepper.

3. **Coat.** Set out three plates: flour, beaten eggs and breadcrumbs. Dredge each cutlet in flour, pull it through the egg, then press it into the breadcrumbs on both sides.

4. **Fry.** Heat a 1–1.5 cm layer of oil in a wide pan to 170–180 °C: a pinch of breadcrumbs should sizzle straight away without burning. Fry the schnitzels for 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden, on medium-high rather than full heat. Drain them on paper towels.

5. **Mash.** Drain the potatoes and mash them with the warm milk and butter. Season with salt and a pinch of nutmeg, and beat until fluffy.

6. **Serve.** Serve the schnitzel with the mash and a lemon wedge, with a cucumber salad if you like.
