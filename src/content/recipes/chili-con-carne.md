1. **Toast and soak the chiles.** Cut the stems off the dried chiles, shake out the seeds and tear them flat. Toast them in a dry pan over medium heat for 30 seconds per side, until fragrant and pliable but not burnt. Put them in a bowl, pour over the hot stock and leave for 20 minutes.

2. **Make the chile paste.** Blend the softened chiles with their stock and the chipotles until completely smooth.

3. **Brown the beef.** Pat the beef dry and season with 1 tsp of the salt. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a large heavy pot over high heat and brown the beef in 3 batches, about 4 minutes per batch, until well browned on at least two sides. Don't crowd the pot or it will steam. Set aside.

4. **Cook the aromatics.** Lower the heat to medium, add the rest of the oil and the onions, and cook for 8 minutes, scraping up the browned bits, until soft and golden. Add the garlic, cumin, oregano, paprika and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute, stirring.

5. **Braise.** Pour in the chile paste and crushed tomatoes, return the beef with its juices, and bring to a simmer. Cover with the lid slightly ajar and cook on the lowest heat (or in the oven at 150 °C) for 2.5 hours, stirring now and then, until the beef breaks apart with a spoon. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.

6. **Add the beans.** Stir in the beans and the chocolate and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes, until the sauce is thick and glossy. Season with the lime juice and salt to taste.

7. **Cook the rice.** During the last 20 minutes, rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet.

8. **Serve.** Ladle the chili over the rice and top with sour cream, cheddar, cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
