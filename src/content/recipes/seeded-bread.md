1. **Dissolve the yeast.** In a large bowl, stir the yeast and honey into the lukewarm water.
2. **Make the dough.** Add both flours, the salt, the oil and most of the seeds (keep a few for the top). Bring it together into a sticky dough and knead for about 5 minutes, until smoother.
3. **First rise.** Cover the bowl with a tea towel and leave somewhere warm for about 1 hour, until doubled in size.
4. **Shape.** Tip the dough onto a floured board, knead briefly and shape into a round loaf. Put it on a lined baking tray, brush with water and sprinkle with the remaining seeds. Heat the oven to 230 °C with an empty tray on the bottom shelf.
5. **Second rise.** Let the loaf rise under the towel for 30 minutes more.
6. **Bake.** Slash the top with a sharp knife, put it in the oven and pour a cup of water onto the bottom tray (steam makes the crust crisp). Bake for 10 minutes at 230 °C, then lower to 200 °C and bake for 25–30 minutes more. It's done when the bottom sounds hollow when tapped.
7. **Cool.** Cool on a rack for at least 20 minutes so the crumb sets.
