1. **Preheat oven.** Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. **Season chicken.** Season each chicken breast with a few drizzles of olive oil, ¼ teaspoon garlic powder, ¼ teaspoon Italian seasoning, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and ground black pepper. Set aside.
3. **Combine ingredients.** In a 9x13-inch baking dish, combine the marinara sauce with the sliced basil. Season with more salt and pepper to taste if desired.
4. **Arrange ingredients.** Place the seasoned chicken on top of the marinara, then arrange the cherry tomato halves around the chicken.
5. **Bake chicken.** Bake, uncovered, for 20-25 minutes, or until the center registers 165°F.
6. **Broil cheese.** Remove from the oven and top with shredded mozzarella. Broil for 2-3 minutes until melted and slightly golden — keep an eye on it, broiling happens fast.
7. **Garnish and serve.** Garnish with chopped parsley and serve while still warm and melty.
