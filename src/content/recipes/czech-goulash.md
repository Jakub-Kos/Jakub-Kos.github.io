1. **Brown the onions slowly.** Melt the lard in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the onions with a pinch of salt and cook for 25 minutes, stirring every few minutes and adding a splash of water if they start to catch, until deep golden brown and jammy.

2. **Add the paprika.** Take the pot off the heat and stir in the sweet and hot paprika; off the heat it won't burn and turn bitter. Stir for 30 seconds until the onions are brick red.

3. **Braise the meat in its own juices.** Put the pot back over medium-high heat, add the beef, caraway and tomato paste, and stir until the meat is coated and no longer raw on the outside. Cover and let it braise in its own juices for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. This concentrates the flavour before any stock goes in.

4. **Add the stock and simmer.** Pour in about a third of the stock, add the bay leaves, salt and pepper, and bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook on low for 2 hours, adding the rest of the stock gradually as it reduces, until the beef is very tender and falls apart when pressed.

5. **Finish.** Stir in the crushed garlic and marjoram. Whisk the flour into a few spoonfuls of cold water and stir it in, then simmer for 10 minutes more so it just binds the sauce. It should stay loose and saucy, not thick. Fish out the bay leaves and season with salt.

6. **Cook the rice.** While the goulash finishes, rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet.

7. **Serve.** Spoon the goulash over the rice and top with a few raw onion rings and a pinch of marjoram.
