1. **Activate yeast.** Warm the milk to about 110°F, then transfer to the bowl of a stand mixer (or a large bowl if using a hand mixer). Mix in half the sugar and the yeast. Set aside for about 5 minutes, or until the yeast is bubbly.
2. **Mix dough.** Whisk in the eggs, vanilla, salt, and remaining sugar, then whisk in half the flour to make a smooth paste.
3. **Knead dough.** Add the melted butter and mix once more, then add the remaining flour and attach a dough hook. Run the mixer on medium speed for 5-7 minutes, or until the dough comes together, is smooth, and doesn't stick to a finger pressed lightly against it.
4. **First rise.** Transfer the dough to a large, lightly oiled bowl, cover, and place somewhere warm to rise for 1.5-2 hours, or until doubled in size.
5. **Make topping.** Mix the flour and powdered sugar together in a medium bowl, then add the butter and vanilla. Work together with a spatula or clean hand until a smooth paste forms.
6. **Shape dough.** Lightly flour the counter and invert the dough onto it, then divide into 12 equal pieces (about 90g each). Cup each piece in your hand and move it in small circles on the unfloured counter to shape into a round roll.
7. **Top buns.** Portion out 1½ tbsp balls of topping (about 30g each). Flatten into thin disks large enough to cover a bun, then place over the bun. Use a very sharp knife to cut the concha shell pattern into the top.
8. **Second rise & preheat.** Cover loosely with plastic wrap and let the conchas rise for about 30 minutes, or until roughly doubled in size. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
9. **Bake.** Bake at 350°F for 18-20 minutes, or until the bottoms are golden.
