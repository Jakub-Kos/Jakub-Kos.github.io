1. **Marinate the chicken.** Toss the chicken thighs with the lemon zest, grated garlic, olive oil and salt. Leave for 20 minutes while you make the pesto (or up to a day in the fridge).

2. **Make the pesto.** Pulse the garlic, pine nuts and coarse salt in a small food processor until finely chopped. Add the basil and pulse in short bursts, scraping down, until finely chopped. Pulse in the parmesan and pecorino, then drizzle in the olive oil while pulsing until you have a loose, bright green paste. (With a mortar: pound the garlic and salt first, then the pine nuts, then the basil a handful at a time, then stir in the cheese and oil.) Press cling film onto the surface so it stays green.

3. **Sear the chicken.** Heat a heavy pan over medium-high heat, add the thighs smooth side down and cook without moving for 6 minutes until deeply browned, then flip and cook for 5 minutes more, until cooked through. Squeeze over half the lemon juice and rest the chicken on a board for 5 minutes, then slice.

4. **Blister the tomatoes.** In the same pan, cook the cherry tomatoes over high heat for 3 minutes, shaking, until they blister and start to burst. Season with a pinch of salt.

5. **Cook the pasta, potatoes and beans.** Bring a big pot of well-salted water to a boil. Add the potato cubes and the pasta together. When the pasta has 6 minutes left, add the green beans. Before draining, scoop out a mug of the pasta water.

6. **Toss with the pesto.** Drain and return everything to the pot, off the heat. Add the pesto and a splash of pasta water and toss vigorously until the sauce turns creamy and coats every strand; the potato starch helps. Add more water if it looks tight.

7. **Serve.** Divide between warm plates and top with the sliced chicken, the blistered tomatoes, the rest of the lemon juice and more parmesan.
