1. **Make the starter (kvásek).** Sift the flour into a large bowl and make a well in the middle. Crumble in the yeast and sprinkle it with 1 tbsp of the sugar. Melt the butter (and lard, if using) in a small pan and pour in the milk, which cools it down to lukewarm. Pour a little of it over the yeast, stir it in, dust with a little flour, cover with a tea towel and leave somewhere warm for about 15 minutes, until bubbly.

2. **Make the dough.** Add the rest of the milk and butter, the egg and yolk, the rest of the sugar, the vanilla sugar, lemon zest and salt. Knead with a mixer (or by hand) into a smooth dough that comes away from the bowl.

3. **Let it rise.** Dust the dough with flour, cover with the tea towel and leave in a warm place for at least 1.5 hours, knocking it back and kneading it briefly twice along the way.

4. **Make the poppy seed filling.** Bring the milk, sugar and a pinch each of cinnamon and cloves to a boil. Stir in the poppy seeds, turn down the heat and stir until thick, about 5 minutes. Take it off the heat and stir in the rum. Let it cool.

5. **Make the povidla filling.** Stir the povidla with the rum and a pinch each of cinnamon and cloves.

6. **Cut the dough.** Brush a deep baking tin or ovenproof dish generously with melted lard. Roll the risen dough into a rectangle about 0.5 cm thick and cut it into 24 squares with a pastry wheel or knife.

7. **Fill and shape.** Flatten each square a little, put a teaspoon of filling in the middle, pull the dough up around it and pinch it shut. Roll it seam side down on the worktop to seal it tight. Set the buns in the tin seam side down, just touching, not squashed, and brush each one with lard. Fill each row with a different filling so you know which is which.

8. **Second rise.** Leave the buns in the tin for 30 minutes to rise again. Meanwhile heat the oven to 170 °C.

9. **Bake.** Bake for about 30 minutes, until golden.

10. **Finish.** Brush the hot buns with the melted butter mixed with a splash of rum and let them cool. Dust generously with powdered sugar before serving.
