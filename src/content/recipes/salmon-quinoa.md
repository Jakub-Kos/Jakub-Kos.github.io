1. **Roast the sweet potatoes.** Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Toss the sweet potato cubes with the olive oil, smoked paprika and a good pinch of salt, spread them out on a lined tray without crowding, and roast for 25 minutes, turning once halfway, until caramelised at the edges.

2. **Prep the salmon.** Pat the fillets completely dry with paper towels, especially the skin, and season both sides with the salt. Leave them on a plate, skin up and uncovered, in the fridge while you get on with everything else.

3. **Cook the quinoa.** Rinse the quinoa well in a sieve (it removes the bitter coating). Toast it in a dry saucepan for 2 minutes until it smells nutty, add the stock and a pinch of salt, bring to a boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Take it off the heat and leave it covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and stir in the lemon zest, half the lemon juice, the parsley and the olive oil.

4. **Make the sauce.** Stir the yogurt with the dill, grated garlic, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.

5. **Cook the mushrooms and spinach.** Heat a little oil in a wide pan over high heat and fry the mushrooms without stirring for 3 minutes, until browned, then add the red onion and cook for 4 more minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then the spinach, and toss until just wilted. Season with salt and pepper and keep warm.

6. **Sear the salmon.** Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Lay the fillets in skin side down, away from you, and press each one flat with a spatula for 15 seconds so the skin doesn't curl. Cook without moving for 4 minutes, until the skin is crisp and the flesh is opaque about three-quarters of the way up. Flip, add the butter, and baste for 1 minute.

7. **Serve.** Spoon quinoa onto warm plates, add the sweet potatoes and the mushrooms and spinach, and set the salmon on top, skin up so it stays crisp. Finish with the rest of the lemon juice and a spoonful of the dill sauce.
