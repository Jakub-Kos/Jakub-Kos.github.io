1. **Marinate the chicken.** Whisk the lime and orange juice, olive oil, garlic, cumin, paprika, chili, oregano, sugar and salt. Set aside 2 tbsp for the vegetables, then toss the chicken in the rest. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, ideally 2–4 hours.

2. **Make the pico de gallo.** Mix the tomatoes, red onion, half the cilantro and the lime juice with a pinch of salt. Let it sit while you cook so the flavours come together.

3. **Make the guacamole.** Mash the avocados roughly with a fork, leaving some chunks. Stir in the lime juice, red onion, jalapeño, the rest of the cilantro and salt to taste. Press cling film onto the surface so it doesn't brown.

4. **Sear the chicken.** Heat a cast-iron or heavy pan over high heat until it just starts to smoke. Shake off the excess marinade and lay the thighs flat. Cook without moving for 5 minutes until charred, flip and cook for 5 minutes more, until cooked through (75 °C inside). Rest on a board for 5 minutes.

5. **Char the peppers and onions.** Wipe out the pan, add the oil and keep the heat high. Cook the peppers and onion for 7 minutes, tossing only now and then, until charred in places but still with some bite. Stir in the reserved marinade for the last 30 seconds.

6. **Warm the tortillas.** Heat the tortillas one at a time in a dry pan, or briefly over a gas flame, for 20 seconds per side, and keep them wrapped in a tea towel.

7. **Serve.** Slice the chicken into strips against the grain and toss it with the peppers and onions. Bring everything to the table in the pan, with the tortillas, guacamole, pico de gallo, sour cream and cheese, and let everyone build their own.
