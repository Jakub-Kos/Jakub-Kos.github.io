1. **Start the potatoes.** Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Toss the potato chunks with the oil and salt and spread on a tray in one layer.
2. **Roast.** Roast for 35–45 minutes, turning them once halfway, until golden and crisp. Start the chicken about 15 minutes after the potatoes go in.
3. **Season the chicken.** Pat the thighs dry and season with salt, pepper, paprika and garlic powder.
4. **Crisp the skin.** Put the thighs skin-side down in a dry or lightly oiled pan on medium heat (about 6 of 9 on a Danish hob). Cook for 12–15 minutes without moving them, until the skin is deep golden and releases easily.
5. **Cook through.** Flip, turn down to medium-low and cook for 10–12 minutes more — covering the pan helps them cook through.
6. **Rest and serve.** Rest the chicken for 5 minutes while the potatoes finish, then serve together.
