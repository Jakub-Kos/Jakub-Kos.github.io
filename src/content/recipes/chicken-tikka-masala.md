1. **Marinate the chicken.** Whisk the yogurt, salt, garam masala, paprika, grated garlic and grated ginger in a bowl until smooth. Toss the chicken in it until every piece is coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 10 minutes, ideally overnight.

2. **Sear the chicken.** Heat a thin layer of oil in a 25 cm pan over medium-high heat until it's nearly smoking. Sear the chicken for 2–3 minutes per side until browned all over, working in batches so the pan isn't crowded. Move it to a plate or tray; it doesn't need to be cooked through yet.

3. **Soften the aromatics.** Turn the heat down to medium and add a bit more oil (and the butter). Add the sliced onion, ginger and garlic with a pinch of salt and cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft.

4. **Toast the spices.** Stir in the paprika, cayenne, garam masala and sugar and cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.

5. **Make the sauce.** Add the crushed tomatoes, stir, and simmer for 5 minutes to thicken slightly. For a smooth restaurant-style sauce, blend it for 1–2 minutes and pour it back into the pan (optional).

6. **Cook the chicken in the sauce.** Lower the heat, add all the chicken with its juices, and stir to coat. Bring it back to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through.

7. **Finish with cream.** Stir in the cream and let it simmer gently for 7–8 minutes until thick and velvety. Season with salt to taste.

8. **Serve.** Spoon over rice and scatter with cilantro.
