1. **Season the pork.** Toss the pork chunks with the salt. If you have time, do this the night before and keep it in the fridge; otherwise give it at least 30 minutes.

2. **Start the confit.** Melt the lard in a heavy pot or Dutch oven just big enough to hold the pork in a snug layer. Add the pork, squeeze in the orange juice and drop in the squeezed halves, then add the onion, garlic, bay leaves, cinnamon, oregano, cumin, milk and enough of the water to come about three-quarters of the way up the meat.

3. **Simmer low and slow.** Bring to a gentle bubble, then turn the heat to low and cook uncovered for 2.5 hours, turning the pieces now and then. The water slowly evaporates and the pork ends up gently frying in the fat. It's ready when a piece falls apart when pressed with a spoon. (Or do this step uncovered in the oven at 150 °C.)

4. **Make the pickled onions.** While the pork cooks, toss the red onion with the lime juice and salt in a jar or bowl. Leave for at least 30 minutes, stirring once; they turn bright pink.

5. **Make the salsa roja.** Char the tomatoes, jalapeños, onion slice and garlic in a dry pan over high heat (or under the grill) for about 10 minutes, turning, until blistered and blackened in spots. Peel the garlic, stem the jalapeños (seed them for a milder salsa) and blend everything with the lime juice, cilantro and a good pinch of salt until chunky-smooth.

6. **Crisp the carnitas.** When the liquid is gone and only fat remains, fish out the onion, orange, bay leaves and cinnamon. Turn the heat to medium-high and fry the pork for 10–15 minutes, turning occasionally, until deep golden and crisp at the edges. Lift it out with a slotted spoon and pull it into coarse chunks with two forks. For extra crunch, fry the shredded meat in batches in a wide pan with 2–3 tbsp of the fat for 3 minutes.

7. **Warm the tortillas.** Heat the tortillas one by one in a dry pan for about 30 seconds per side until soft and lightly spotted, and keep them wrapped in a tea towel.

8. **Serve.** Stack two tortillas per taco, street style, and pile on the carnitas. Let everyone add onion, cilantro, feta, pickled onions, salsa and a squeeze of lime.
