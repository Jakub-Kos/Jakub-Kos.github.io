---
1. Slice the onions thin. Cook them low and slow in olive oil for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply caramelized.
2. Mince the garlic and add it for the last 2 minutes of cooking the onions.
3. Meanwhile, boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water.
4. Toss the drained pasta into the onions with a splash of pasta water to loosen it into a sauce.
5. Serve topped with grated parmesan.
