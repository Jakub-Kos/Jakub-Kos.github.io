1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan) and line a baking tray with parchment.
2. **Prep the vegetables.** Halve the potatoes (no need to peel), slice the carrots into rounds and cut the onion into wedges. Toss on the tray with 2 tbsp of the oil and some of the salt and pepper, and spread in one layer.
3. **Roast the vegetables alone.** Roast for 20 minutes, until the potatoes are nearly tender.
4. **Add the salmon and broccoli.** Take the tray out, stir the vegetables and push them to the edges. Lay the salmon skin-side down in the middle with the broccoli florets around it. Drizzle with the rest of the oil, season, sprinkle with one chopped garlic clove and lay a few lemon slices on the salmon.
5. **Finish roasting.** Roast for 12–15 minutes more, until the salmon flakes easily with a fork.
6. **Make the sauce.** Meanwhile mix the yogurt with the remaining garlic (crushed), the honey, the mustard, the juice of the rest of the lemon and a pinch of salt.
7. **Serve.** Plate the salmon and vegetables with a spoonful of sauce.
