1. **Roast the potatoes.** Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Toss the halved potatoes with the olive oil and salt and roast them cut side down on a tray for 30 minutes, turning once, until golden and crisp.

2. **Make the tartar sauce.** Stir the mayonnaise and yogurt together with the gherkins and their brine, the mustard, the onion and the lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper and chill until serving.

3. **Set up the coating.** Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt in a shallow dish. Put the flour and the breadcrumbs on two plates.

4. **Coat the cheese.** Dip each slice of cheese briefly in cold water so the flour sticks. Coat it in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs. Then dip it in the egg and breadcrumbs a second time, pressing the crumbs on well and sealing the edges so the cheese can't escape.

5. **Fry.** Heat a 1.5 cm layer of oil in a frying pan to 170–180 °C, until a pinch of breadcrumbs sizzles straight away. Fry the cheese quickly, about 1–2 minutes per side, until deep golden. Drain on paper towels.

6. **Serve.** Serve straight away while the cheese is molten, with the potatoes scattered with chives, a big spoonful of tartar sauce and a few cherry tomatoes.
