1. **Rinse the rice.** Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs almost clear, then let it drain well; it needs to be fairly dry to fry.

2. **Blend the tomato base.** Blend the chopped tomatoes, the ¼ onion and the 2 garlic cloves until smooth. You should have about 200 ml.

3. **Toast the rice.** Heat the oil in a wide saucepan over medium heat. Add the rice and fry, stirring often, for 6 minutes, until the grains turn opaque and golden in spots.

4. **Simmer the rice.** Pour in the tomato purée and fry it with the rice for 2 minutes, until it darkens and mostly cooks off. Add the hot stock and the salt, stir once, and bring to a boil. Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 15 minutes without lifting the lid. Take it off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

5. **Brown the beef.** While the rice cooks, heat a splash of oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Add the beef in one flat layer and leave it for 3 minutes until a brown crust forms, then break it up and cook until browned all over. Push it to one side, add the onion and bell pepper and cook for 5 minutes until soft.

6. **Bloom the spices.** Stir in the garlic, cumin, paprika, oregano, chipotle and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant and brick red.

7. **Simmer.** Add the crushed tomatoes and the beans, season with salt and simmer gently for 10 minutes until thick. Finish with the lime juice.

8. **Make the pico de gallo.** Mix the diced tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro and lime juice with a pinch of salt.

9. **Build the bowls.** Spoon the rice into bowls, top with the beef and beans, and finish with avocado, pico de gallo, a spoonful of sour cream, cheddar and a wedge of lime.
