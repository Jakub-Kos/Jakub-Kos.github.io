1. **Prep the vegetables.** Wash the squash, halve it, scoop out the seeds with a spoon and cut it into 2–3 cm cubes, skin on. Peel and chop the potatoes, carrot, onion and garlic; grate the ginger.
2. **Sauté.** Heat the oil or butter in a pot and soften the onion over low heat until translucent, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and fry for 1 minute more.
3. **Simmer.** Add the squash, potatoes and carrot, pour in the stock and bring to a boil. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes, until the squash and potatoes fall apart when pierced with a fork.
4. **Blend.** Blend smooth with a stick blender. Stir in the coconut milk, simmer for 3 minutes, and season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
5. **Toast the seeds.** Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry pan for about 3 minutes, until they start to pop and smell nutty.
6. **Serve.** Ladle into bowls, scatter with the seeds, and add a swirl of cream or pumpkin seed oil if you have it.
