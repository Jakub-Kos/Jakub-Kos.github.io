## Knowledge
Hi, as of late I have started feeling that `knowledge` that I once had is slowly fading away. I tend to have good short-term memory 
and analytical skills, so understanding most topics is not a problem. Problem is the time and energy one needs to put into learning 
something new and then loosing that. That's why I wanted to build a space where I can keep track of my `knowledge` and maybe share it 
with others.

<img src="https://pbs.twimg.com/media/FgprAIjXoAA1GaK.jpg" alt="Knowledge Guy">

I have wanted to create a personal website for a long time, and I have tried multiple times, last year I created one to host nice 
visualizations for `Robotics` class I took. By the overall structure was a mess and I wanted to start fresh and organized from the 
start. After searching for a while I found [Astro](https://astro.build/), a static site generator that is very easy to use and has a lot of integrations. We will see how it goes this time...

## The style
The style of this website will be a bit different than what I tend to see online. I wanted to create `knowledge` base of stuff I find,
learn or just want to keep track of. Most sites I see are either blogs, portfolios or some other about-me type of stuff. That's all 
good, this Page will eventually also have some blog stuff, some portfolios and project but the main focus is to box my `knowledge`
(which blog, porfolios and projects are in a way, the aim is to present yourself).

I was thinking for a while how I can connect this all, my approach is this. I like me some Wiki and Obsidian style mind maps, so that's what I'm gonna do. The key thing here will be the Wiki and the Knowledge Map, but there will be also the Blog section and so on.

## The blog
This struck me while setting things up. I know I wanted to make some diary since ever, to reference old stuff or just to see how
I was thinking in the past. My motivations and views on stuff constantly change, so that's one reason. But now when I'm writing this,
I know and feel that the structure of the sentences is not ideal, but I kind of like it this way, just writing down what I'm thinking.
The thing is that I've been using LLMs for helping me write stuff that I almost forgot to just do it myself. And I also feel like this
looks more genuine, maybe not perfect, but that's fine. Yeah and also I viewed blogs as a relic of the old internet, but it has it's
perks. If you're still reading this, hi, hello, try it! I sure hope this is not the last time I am doing this.
